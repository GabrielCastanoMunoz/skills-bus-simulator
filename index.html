School Bus Air Brake Simulator

Interactive practice • Version 1 • Educational demonstration
Training scenario

Explore how air pressure changes with the engine, service brake and parking brake. Pressure values and response rates are illustrative, not vehicle specifications.

PRIMARY AIR
90 PSI
SECONDARY AIR
90 PSI

Driver controls

Hold the service brake button to apply it. Pump brake releases a small amount of air each click.

Instrument indicators

Engine: OFF

Parking brake: SET

Low-air warning: OFF

Service brake: RELEASED

Learning notes

  1. Start the engine to observe pressure buildup.
  2. Switch off the engine and pump the brake to observe pressure loss.
  3. Watch the low-pressure warning and automatic spring brake behavior.
  4. Compare your observations against the manufacturer's procedures and current CDL training requirements.
This is not a certified training device, CDL skills examination, or substitute for instructor-supervised vehicle inspection. Actual thresholds, leakage limits, test steps and timing depend on vehicle and applicable regulations.
